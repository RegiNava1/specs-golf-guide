## Judging strategy
Aim for a simple, fully functioning spatial golf practice experience. Prioritize design, technical implementation, innovation, and demonstration equally until the latest published judging weights have been verified. Do not claim real-distance accuracy, green contours, ball tracking or supported sensors without direct evidence in tool documentation and tests.

## Candidate MVP, pending official planning exercise
